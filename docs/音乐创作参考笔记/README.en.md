# Music-creation reference notes

This directory publicly accompanies Tianlai with one creative reference on
music creation, arranging, performance expression, and listening evaluation.
It is intended for human creators and participating AI agents, helping turn
“what should this feel like?” into judgments that can be discussed, edited,
heard, and reviewed.

This material is a **non-normative creative reference**:

- it is not a technical contract that the engine, schemas, MCP interface, or
  release process must enforce;
- it does not choose melody, style, or aesthetics for the creator, and following
  it does not guarantee that a result will sound good;
- adoption is entirely the creator's choice; declining to follow it triggers no
  project penalty, loss of license, or feature restriction;
- metrics help find problems and organize evidence, while human listening keeps
  final authority over a particular work; and
- it may be revised through practice, listening review, and community feedback,
  without presenting the current version as a universal law.

The project author retained and incorporated this material into Tianlai, with
AI assistance used for parts of the research and organization. Any third-party
paper, standard, website, product, or other material mentioned remains subject
to its own rights and license. The text license below neither enlarges those
permissions nor implies endorsement by the referenced source or by Tianlai.

## Current note

1. [Tianlai Music Constitution v0.2](天籁音乐宪法-v0.2.en.md)

   It distinguishes a formed composition from a formed world, and emergence
   from consequence. Material may precede a verbal reason; major structural
   choices bear proportionate history and consequences, while companion lines,
   breath, resonance, and silence may first earn a place through whole-work
   listening. Clauses generate questions rather than answers.

## Recommended use

1. Let seeds occur before gradually identifying the work's identity, direction,
   and already-established relationships.
2. Select only a small number of relevant principles and use clauses as questions,
   not answers.
3. Apply causal scrutiny to major, conspicuous, irreversible choices; grant small,
   reversible material the right to be tried in the complete work first.
4. Mark places where a companion line, breath, resonance, spatial change, or
   deliberate emptiness may be tried. Zero additions is a valid result; never
   fill a quota or a fixed bar pattern.
5. Prefer whole-work addition/removal comparison for peripheral details. Without
   actual audition, record only a hypothesis rather than claiming improvement.
6. Promote recurring, reproducible experience into a later revision instead of
   rewriting general guidance from one accidental listening impression.
