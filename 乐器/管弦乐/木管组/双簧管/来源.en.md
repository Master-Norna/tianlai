# Oboe Sample Source

This entry point uses **Virtual Playing Orchestra Standard Orchestra 3.3 (2026-06-27)** and **Wave Files 3.2 (2026-06-27)**.

- Curation and mapping: Paul Battersby, Virtual Playing Orchestra, <https://virtualplaying.com/virtual-playing-orchestra/>
- Actual sample sublibrary: `libs/SSO/Samples/Oboe/`
- Upstream samples: Sonatina Symphonic Orchestra, Creative Commons Sampling Plus 1.0; mixed-library terms are governed by `Documentation/license.htm` retained with VPO
- SFZ files used: `oboe-SOLO-normal-mod-wheel.sfz`, `oboe-SOLO-sustain.sfz`, `oboe-SOLO-staccato.sfz`, and `oboe-SOLO-accent.sfz`

VPO's notice permits music made with the library. Anyone repacking, modifying, or distributing the samples or SFZ files themselves must still comply item by item with Sampling Plus 1.0, attribution requirements, and VPO's mixed-library notice. The source release does not include audio assets.

See [资源核验.json](资源核验.json) for exact hashes of the 4 SFZ files, aggregate hash for the 9 WAV files, licenses, and version evidence; reproduce them with `核验资源.py`. A general-purpose SoundFont is retained only as an explicit failure fallback.
