# Bassoon Sample Source

This entry point uses **Virtual Playing Orchestra Standard Orchestra 3.3 (2026-06-27)** and **Wave Files 3.2 (2026-06-27)**.

- Curation and mapping: Paul Battersby, Virtual Playing Orchestra, <https://virtualplaying.com/virtual-playing-orchestra/>
- Sustain samples: `libs/SSO/Samples/Bassoon/`, Sonatina Symphonic Orchestra, Creative Commons Sampling Plus 1.0
- Short-attack layers: `libs/Iowa/samples/Bassoon-solo/`, University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios; the bundled statement permits downloading and project use without restriction
- `bassoon-SOLO-staccato.sfz` states that its mapping is CC BY-SA 4.0
- Four mappings used: `bassoon-SOLO-normal-mod-wheel.sfz`, `bassoon-SOLO-sustain.sfz`, `bassoon-SOLO-staccato.sfz`, and `bassoon-SOLO-accent.sfz`

This is mixed licensing and must not be labeled as a single CC0 license or merely “free.” Rendering music with the samples is different from redistributing the sample library; redistribution must retain the license and attribution evidence for VPO, SSO, Iowa, and the mapping files. The source release does not include audio assets.

See [资源核验.json](资源核验.json) for exact hashes of the 4 SFZ files, 79 deduplicated WAV files, `Documentation/license.htm`, the Iowa license copy, and version evidence; reproduce them with `核验资源.py`. A general-purpose SoundFont is available only as an explicit failure fallback.
