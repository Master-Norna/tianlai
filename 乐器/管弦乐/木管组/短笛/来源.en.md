# Piccolo Sample Source

This entry point uses **Virtual Playing Orchestra Standard Orchestra 3.3 (2026-06-27)** and **Wave Files 3.2 (2026-06-27)**.

- Curation and mapping: Paul Battersby, Virtual Playing Orchestra, <https://virtualplaying.com/virtual-playing-orchestra/>
- Actual sample sublibrary: `libs/SSO/Samples/Piccolo/`
- Upstream samples: Sonatina Symphonic Orchestra, Creative Commons Sampling Plus 1.0; mixed-library terms are governed by `Documentation/license.htm`
- SFZ files used: `piccolo-SOLO-normal-mod-wheel.sfz`, `piccolo-SOLO-sustain.sfz`, `piccolo-SOLO-staccato.sfz`, and `piccolo-SOLO-accent.sfz`

VPO's notice permits music made with the library. Anyone repacking, modifying, or distributing the SFZ/WAV resources themselves must still comply with Sampling Plus 1.0, attribution requirements, and VPO's mixed-library notice. The lightweight source package does not include audio assets.

See [资源核验.json](资源核验.json) for exact hashes of the 4 SFZ files, aggregate hash for the 10 WAV files, licenses, and version evidence; reproduce them with `核验资源.py`. A general-purpose SoundFont is available only as an explicit failure fallback.
