# Violin Sample Source

The violin uses the solo-violin samples and standard SFZ mappings from **Virtual Playing Orchestra 3.3**.

- Curation and maintenance: Paul Battersby
- Upstream: https://virtualplaying.com/virtual-playing-orchestra/
- Wave package: Virtual Playing Orchestra 3.2 Wave Files
- Mappings: Virtual Playing Orchestra 3.3 Standard SFZ Scripts
- Articulations: sustain, normal, staccato, accent, pizzicato, tremolo

This library combines sources including Sonatina Symphonic Orchestra, No Budget Orchestra, VSCO 2 Community Edition, and the University of Iowa, with different Creative Commons terms applying to specific files. Upstream permits commercial use of finished music made with the library. Repacking or distributing the samples requires retaining appropriate attribution and keeping distribution free of charge. Audio assets are not placed under this project's source control.

The original README and license files from the upstream archives are retained with the samples. If the samples themselves are distributed in the future, the license must be checked source by source rather than relying solely on this summary.

The license status of this entry point is `grandfathered`: the installer obtains only the pinned complete package through VPO's official download chain, extracts it locally without modification, and verifies its 1,922-file tree. Tianlai does not mirror, extract subsets from, or repackage the WAV files. The current formal SOLO/SEC entries use a combination of NBO/SSO; they are not the University of Iowa violin used by historical versions, so the Iowa unrestricted-use statement cannot be applied directly to the current samples. The complete review is documented in [`VPO 音源许可与安装说明`](../../../../docs/VPO音源许可与安装说明.en.md).
