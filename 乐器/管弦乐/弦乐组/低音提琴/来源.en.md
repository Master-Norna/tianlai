# Double Bass Sample Source

This entry point uses the solo-double-bass SFZ files from **Virtual Playing Orchestra Standard Orchestra 3.3 (2026-06-27)** with **Wave Files 3.2 (2026-06-27)**.

- Curation and mapping: Paul Battersby, Virtual Playing Orchestra
- Upstream project: <https://virtualplaying.com/virtual-playing-orchestra/>
- Sustain and staccato samples: `libs/VSCO2-CE/Strings/Solo Contrabass/`, VSCO 2 Community Edition, CC0 1.0
- Pizzicato samples: `libs/Iowa/samples/Bass-solo/`, University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios; the bundled license record states that they may be downloaded free of charge and used in any project without restriction
- Pizzicato SFZ mapping: Paul Battersby; the file header states CC BY-SA 4.0
- SFZ files used by this project: `bass-SOLO-sustain.sfz`, `bass-SOLO-staccato.sfz`, `bass-SOLO-pizzicato.sfz`, `bass-SOLO-accent.sfz`

The upstream notice permits commercial use of music made with the library. Anyone repacking, modifying, or distributing the samples or SFZ files themselves must also check VPO's mixed licensing, VSCO2-CE CC0, the Iowa statement, and the pizzicato mapping's CC BY-SA 4.0, and must retain the full upstream licenses and attribution. The source release does not include these audio assets.

The former GeneralUser GS/TimGM `fallback` is available only as a failure fallback explicitly selected by the user. The current default manifest does not switch to GM automatically or silently and does not label the fallback timbre as a candidate.

Exact SFZ SHA-256 values, the aggregate SHA-256 for 55 deduplicated samples, license-file hashes, and the aggregation algorithm are available in [资源核验.json](资源核验.json).
