# French Horn Sample Source

This entry point uses `french-horn-SOLO-sustain.sfz` and `french-horn-SOLO-staccato.sfz` from **Virtual Playing Orchestra Standard Orchestra 3.3 (2026-06-27)** with **Wave Files 3.2 (2026-06-27)**.

- Primary solo samples: University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios; the bundled statement says they may be downloaded and used in any project without restriction.
- Samples filling missing notes: samples by Mattias Westlund under VPO `libs/SSO/Samples/Horn/`, CC BY-SA 3.0.
- SFZ curation and mapping: Paul Battersby; file headers state CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Upstream project: <https://virtualplaying.com/virtual-playing-orchestra/>.

According to the upstream notice, rendered music may be used in commercial projects. Anyone repacking, modifying, or distributing the samples or SFZ files themselves must retain the full upstream licenses and attribution and comply separately with each license in the mixture.
