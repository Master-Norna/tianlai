# Trumpet Sample Source

This entry point uses `trumpet-SOLO-sustain.sfz` and `trumpet-SOLO-staccato.sfz` from **Virtual Playing Orchestra Standard Orchestra 3.3 (2026-06-27)** with **Wave Files 3.2 (2026-06-27)**.

- Solo samples: University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios; the bundled statement says they may be downloaded and used in any project without restriction.
- SFZ curation and mapping: Paul Battersby; file headers state CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Upstream project: <https://virtualplaying.com/virtual-playing-orchestra/>.

According to the upstream notice, rendered music may be used in commercial projects. Redistribution of samples or SFZ files should retain the VPO and Iowa source evidence, full license texts, and attribution. The source release does not include audio assets.
