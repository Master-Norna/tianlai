# Orchestral Cymbals Sample Source

This entry point is pinned to `Percussion/cymbals.sfz` from VPO **Standard Orchestra 3.3 / Wave Files 3.2 (2026-06-27)**. Its 15 deduplicated WAV files cover SSO piatti/rolls and VSCO2-CE suspended-cymbal crashes, two velocity layers × 2 round robins, and short/medium/long crescendos.

- SSO: Creative Commons Sampling Plus 1.0.
- VSCO2 Community Edition: CC0 1.0.
- VPO curation and mapping: Paul Battersby.

The aggregate resource SHA-256 is `a9ec3214ff999947bb84b8adfd6137366ddb285d84fe8233a1dfb6497eb6d256`; see [资源核验.json](资源核验.json) for complete evidence. There is no fixed pitch; the high variants in the SFZ are timbral strikes, not a promise of tunable pitch.
