# Source

> This English translation is provided for reference only. The original
> upstream license and evidence take precedence.

- Upstream project: Greg Sullivan E-Pianos, Yamaha CP80 Electric Grand Piano
- Original URL: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/GregSullivan.E-Pianos
- Adopted version: `8c3e581acda3594b553948ff0222d4f84a698376`
- License: [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/);
  original recordings by Greg Sullivan, with SFZ/FLAC mapping by kinwie. The
  full license and README are both frozen in `资源核验.json`.
- Uses `CP80/CP80.sfz` and its four velocity levels of FLAC files; resource statistics and the aggregate Hash are recomputed by `核验资源.py`.
- Resources are shared with the electric-piano entry. Pinned-commit, no-overwrite installation and license/sample verification are performed by [`../获取GregSullivan电钢琴音源.ps1`](../获取GregSullivan电钢琴音源.ps1).
- The effect chain is project-developed deterministic DSP (`tianlai/dedicated_fx.py`), with parameters frozen in `乐器.json`.
- The upstream SFZ and FLAC bytes remain unchanged. Tianlai first renders 48
  kHz through its project-authored deterministic band-limited resampler, then
  applies stereo chorus. Attribution, the license link, and both change notices
  flow from `乐器.json` into the license sidecar.
- The former VCSL TX81Z FM Piano was retired because the entire instrument sounded one octave too low.
