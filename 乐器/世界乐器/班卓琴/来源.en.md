## Adopted scope

The frozen upstream source contains 58 mapped regions and 58 independent WAV
files. Runtime filtering leaves 55 regions and 55 WAV files in use, with 39
regions participating in round robin. The upstream SFZ has no velocity-layer
conditions, so these samples must not be described as genuine multi-velocity
layers. The playable mapping is MIDI 38-72.

## Semantic limitation

This source samples a six-string guitar-banjo rather than a traditional
five-string banjo. The replacement resolves the former source's license
isolation issue, but it also changes the instrument variant. Scores that depend
on the traditional short fifth string, open tuning, or roll vocabulary must be
distinguished explicitly by the collaboration layer; a six-string instrument
must not be treated as a high-fidelity superset of a five-string one.
