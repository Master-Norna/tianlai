# Source and rights statement

> This English translation is provided for reference only. The original
> upstream license and evidence take precedence.

`乐器.py` in this directory is code developed by the Tianlai project. At
runtime it reads no samples, impulse responses, SoundFont, SFZ, or other
external audio resources. `资源核验.json` freezes the implementation file's
SHA-256 and explicitly records an empty external-resource array. The
implementation is released under the repository-root Apache License 2.0
(Apache-2.0). The structured provenance statement likewise confirms that this
is project-developed DSP containing no third-party samples or other external
audio assets.

The model uses assumptions at the level of public acoustic knowledge:
elliptical two-tone bells have two groups of inharmonic modes that vary with
strike position, and radiation losses generally make lower bells decay longer
than higher bells. The current modal ratios and decay curves are only modeling
parameters for a “representative modern two-tone bell.” They are not
attributed to, and do not claim to reproduce through measurement, the Marquis
Yi of Zeng bells or any other specific museum collection.

The following public materials were consulted before modeling, solely to
confirm the acoustic direction of two-tone behavior, strike positions, and
inharmonic modes:

- [Hubei Provincial Museum: collection introduction to the Marquis Yi of Zeng bells](https://m-www.hbww.org.cn/p/4695.html)
- [Shenzhen Museum: explanation of central- and side-strike areas on bianzhong](https://www.shenzhenmuseum.com/webCollection/collectionDetail?lmType=L0303&resId=b184e070c4ed4df6bb77780a1e5bb8b7)
- [Acoustical Society of America: almond-shaped cross sections and paired modes in Asian bells](https://acoustics.org/pressroom/httpdocs/147th/Rossing_AsianBells2.htm)
- [JASA / PubMed: abstract of theoretical research on two-tone-bell cross sections and pitch relationships](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14514215/)

No audio, program code, or per-bell measurement data was copied from these
materials. The modal tables, decay curves, transients, and stereo parameters
in this project are all representative synthesis parameters designed by the
project.

The first version intentionally omits room reverberation, strongly detuned
near-frequency pairs, LFO, and left/right channel detuning. This avoids using a
long reverb to impersonate a bell body and reduces the risk of persistent
beating being perceived as electrical hum. The weaker modal family for the
other strike position remains short and quiet. The anti-aliasing filter fades
continuously from 0.34 times the sample rate and reaches zero at 0.45 times the
sample rate.
